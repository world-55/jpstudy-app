Privacy Policy

JAPANESE IDOL STUDY · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the app "JAPANESE IDOL STUDY" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it has no user accounts. We do not collect, store or sell personal data on our servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves a few settings on your own device only, such as your chosen language, display options, quiz best scores and whether the full version is unlocked. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Purchases

If you buy the full version, the payment is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Third-party services

The App's lessons and videos are stored inside the App on your device. The App loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Purchases are handled by Google Play. The App does not use advertising or analytics tools.

5. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: idolappmailbox@gmail.com

プライバシーポリシー
